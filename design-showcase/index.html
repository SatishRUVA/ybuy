Start with Fresh Neighborhood.

It makes the local, person-to-person nature of YBuy feel immediate without making the interface feel like an old classifieds site. Location, availability, price, and owner approval stay easy to scan.

It is a design recommendation, not user research. Contemporary Commerce is a strong alternative when fast, utilitarian browsing is the priority.
